# Zazo — Landing Page Content & Messaging Guide

> A consolidated content brief based on the existing Zazo landing-page themes.
>
> Suggested copy below is a starting point, not a verbatim transcription or a verified product specification. Confirm availability, pricing, rewards, financial-partner details, and launch claims before publishing.

---

## 1. What the Landing Page Is About

Zazo is presented as a connected way to receive international money, convert it into local currency, and spend locally.

The central story is:

**Get paid globally. Convert clearly. Spend locally.**

Instead of treating receiving, conversion, spending, and rewards as separate experiences, the landing page presents them as parts of one connected product.

### Core message

> Get paid in USD. Spend in PKR.

### Supporting message

> Bring international income and everyday spending together in one app.

### Main visitor takeaway

“I understand how Zazo could help me receive money, see what I’ll get when converting, and use that money locally.”

### Main conversion goal

Encourage interested visitors to join the waitlist.

### Secondary goal

Explain how the product works and answer the questions that prevent someone from signing up.

---

## 2. Product Story

### The problem

People receiving money internationally may need several services to:

- Receive a payment.
- Hold or manage the balance.
- Convert it into local currency.
- Move it to a local account.
- Pay for everyday purchases.
- Understand the total cost.

This can create fragmented experiences and uncertainty about rates, fees, and the final amount received.

### Zazo’s proposed answer

A more connected experience for receiving money, understanding conversion, and spending locally.

### Content pillars

1. **Global receiving**
   Receive international payments through supported channels.

2. **Conversion clarity**
   Understand the quoted rate and expected amount before confirming.

3. **Local spending**
   Use local-currency funds for supported payments and transfers.

4. **Rewards**
   Explain the reward proposition and its eligibility clearly.

5. **Trust**
   Explain how the service operates, who provides financial services, and what protections apply.

### Messaging hierarchy

Lead with the practical outcome.

Then explain the features.

Then provide evidence, conditions, pricing, and reassurance.

Do not lead with a long list of technical capabilities.

## 10. Conversion Calculator or Worked Example

### Purpose

Make the conversion proposition concrete.

### Suggested heading

> See what your money could become.

### Suggested supporting copy

> Enter an amount to explore the estimated conversion. Review the final quote before confirming a transaction.

### Content fields

- You send.
- Sending currency.
- You receive.
- Receiving currency.
- Exchange rate.
- Applicable fees.
- Last updated.
- Quote expiry, if relevant.

### Important distinction

Use the correct label for the experience:

- **Live quote:** connected to an actual quote service.
- **Estimate:** calculated but not guaranteed.
- **Example:** illustrative, not connected to current rates.

Do not label a static example “Live rate.”

### Suggested example disclaimer

> Illustrative example only. Rates and fees may change. The final amount is shown before confirmation.

### CTA

**Join the Waitlist**

For an available product, replace this with the appropriate approved action.

## 12. Rewards Section

### Purpose

Explain the reward proposition without making it sound unconditional.

### Suggested heading

> More value from eligible everyday activity.

### Suggested supporting copy

> Explore how Zazo rewards may apply to eligible balances, payments, or activity, with the conditions explained clearly.

### Information the section must answer

- What earns a reward?
- Who is eligible?
- Is there a balance requirement?
- Are there levels or tiers?
- Is there a spending cap?
- When is the reward credited?
- Does it expire?
- Which merchants or transactions qualify?
- Can the terms change?

### Existing design theme

The designs connect higher balances or greater use with increased rewards.

That theme requires an approved explanation of the actual reward mechanics.

### Avoid

- “Rewards on every payment” unless universally true.
- Unapproved percentages.
- Guaranteed earnings language.
- Implying that rewards are interest or an investment return unless that is the approved product structure.

### Purpose

Explain when Zazo may replace a fragmented workflow.

### Existing theme

> One app instead of three.

### Safer suggested framing

> Compare the steps, not just the headline.

### Useful comparison topics

- Receiving international payments.
- Seeing a conversion quote.
- Accessing local currency.
- Making local payments.
- Tracking transactions.
- Understanding fees.
- Eligibility for rewards.

### Comparison requirements

Every competitor statement should have:

- A reliable source.
- A review date.
- Relevant country and account context.
- Clear treatment of conditional features.
- Appropriate legal review.

### If evidence is incomplete

Use a workflow comparison instead of named competitors.

For example:

| Task | Fragmented workflow | Zazo proposition |
|---|---|---|
| Receive payment | One service | Supported receiving within the Zazo experience |
| Convert funds | Another step or provider | Conversion quote shown in the journey |
| Spend locally | Separate local payment service | Supported local payments |
| Review activity | Multiple histories | Connected transaction visibility |

The Zazo column still requires product confirmation.

| Action | Fee | Conditions |
|---|---|---|

| Convert currency | Confirm before publishing | Show rate and applicable charges |

### Purpose

- Financial partners and their roles.
- Where balances are held.
- Verification requirements.
- Transaction monitoring.
- Account access and security controls.
- Support and issue resolution.
- Applicable protections and limitations.

### Existing design themes

The designs refer to:

- Licensed and regulated financial partners.
- Encrypted and verified transactions.
- Logging and reconciliation.

These are claims requiring confirmation before publication.

### Writing rules

- Explain what “protected” means.
- Distinguish Zazo’s role from a partner’s role.

- Avoid absolute promises such as “100% safe.”
- Link to relevant legal and security information.

## 16. FAQ Content

### 1. What is Zazo?

**Suggested answer:**

> Zazo is being developed to connect supported international receiving, currency conversion, and local payments in one experience. Availability depends on your location, account eligibility, and the features offered at launch.

### 2. Who can use Zazo?

**Answer should explain:**

- Eligible countries or residency.
- Individual versus business accounts.
- Age requirements.
- Verification requirements.

### 3. Which currencies can I receive?

**Answer should explain:**

- Currently supported currencies.
- Planned currencies.
- Whether receiving and holding availability differ.
- Any account-specific restrictions.

Do not infer support from currencies shown in illustrations.

### 4. How fast does money arrive?

**Suggested answer structure:**

> Arrival times depend on the payment method, sending institution, currency, and any required checks. Show the expected timing for the selected method before the user proceeds.

Replace with approved service-specific details before publication.

### 5. What rate do I get when converting?

**Suggested answer:**

> Review the quoted exchange rate, applicable fees, and expected receiving amount before confirming. Explain whether the quote can expire or change.

### 6. Can I spend directly from the app?

**Answer should explain:**

- Supported local-payment methods.
- Merchant coverage.
- Currency requirements.
- Limits.
- Any verification conditions.

### 7. What does Zazo cost?

Provide an approved summary and link to the full fee schedule.

Do not imply that all activity is free unless that is accurate.

### 8. How do rewards work?

Explain:

- Eligible activity.
- Tiers or balance requirements.
- Caps.
- Timing.
- Exclusions.
- Terms.

### 9. How is my money held?

Explain the actual financial-service structure and partner responsibilities.

Use legally approved wording.

### 10. When will Zazo launch?

Give one consistent answer:

- Confirmed launch date.
- Limited-access status.
- Or waitlist-only status.

Do not invent a date.

### 11. How will I hear about availability?

**Suggested answer:**

> Join the waitlist to receive relevant launch and availability updates.

Only promise communications the team intends and is permitted to send.

## 21. Content Principles

### Lead with outcomes

Explain what the visitor can accomplish before explaining the underlying mechanism.

### Be specific

Prefer:

> See the quoted rate before confirming.

Over:

> Enjoy revolutionary financial freedom.

### Separate current and planned functionality

Use explicit labels:

- Available.
- Limited access.
- Planned.
- Coming soon.

### Keep claims supportable

Numbers, comparisons, fees, protection claims, and availability statements need evidence.

### Make conditions visible

Do not hide important restrictions beneath a broad headline.

### Keep the story consistent

The hero, feature cards, FAQ, pricing, and waitlist should describe the same product state.

### Avoid repetition

Each section should add something:

- Hero: what it is.
- Flow: how it works.
- Benefits: why it matters.
- Product stories: practical detail.
- Pricing: what it costs.
- Security: how it operates.
- FAQ: remaining questions.
- Waitlist: next step.

## 23. One-Paragraph Content Brief

Create a benefit-led Zazo landing page that explains the journey from receiving international money to using it locally. Lead with USD income and PKR spending, then explain account setup, supported receiving methods, conversion transparency, local payments, and rewards. Address freelancers, businesses, remittance recipients, merchants, and local users through recognizable situations. Build trust through clear pricing, accurate partner and security explanations, and practical FAQs. Finish with a focused waitlist invitation. Keep all availability, fee, rate, reward, and protection claims aligned with approved product facts.
